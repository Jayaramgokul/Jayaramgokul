### Hi, I'm Jayaram 👋

I validate and build AI and quantitative models for financial services, in London.

My day job is independent model validation and AI governance: testing whether
models, including LLM-based ones, do what they claim, and whether a regulator
would agree. On the side I build AI tools, and I try to hold them to the same
standard: **use the model for judgement, then make the code check it.**

**Featured work**

| Project | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [**wealth-advisory-validation**](https://github.com/Jayaramgokul/wealth-advisory-validation) | How I validate models used in investment and wealth management, from regulation and the NIST AI RMF to research-based test design: 50 tests, each with its client harm, method and pass criteria, and 26 illustrations on simulated data. |
| [**llm-agent-validation**](https://github.com/Jayaramgokul/llm-agent-validation) | How I validate LLM agents in regulated financial services: sycophancy, red-team failure rates with honest intervals, validating the LLM judge, and calibration. 25 tests drawn from the research, with 14 illustrations. |

| [**macro-stress-test**](https://github.com/Jayaramgokul/macro-stress-test) | US bank loan losses projected through the Fed's 2026 stress scenarios, then backtested: built on data to 2006, the mortgage model would have predicted 37% of the 2008 peak. Streamlit what-if tool. |
